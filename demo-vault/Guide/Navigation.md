# Navigation

- **⌘O** — quick switcher: fuzzy-find any note by name, alias, or path.
  ⇧↵ creates a note with the typed name; ⌘↵ opens in a new tab.
- **⌘P** — command palette: every command, with its hotkey.
- **⌘⌥←/→** — back and forward through each tab's history.

## Tabs and splits

Notes open in tabs (⌘T new, ⌘W close, ⌃Tab cycle). **Drag a tab** onto
another pane's edge to split the window (or use ⌘\ / ⌘⇧\); drag onto a
tab bar to reorder or move between panes. The layout — panes, tabs,
sidebar widths — is saved per vault and restored on reopen.

⌘B and ⌘⇧B toggle the left and right sidebars.

See also: [[Panels]], [[Settings and Hotkeys]].
